    How it works

    1. Paste logs or open a file. Nothing is uploaded; parsing runs on your device.
    2. It reads SSH, sudo, PAM and account lines, counts them, and flags IPs with many failures or a login that succeeded after failures.
    3. Use the next-step list as a starting checklist, then confirm on the server.

    Limits: lite analyzer for common OpenSSH, sudo and PAM message formats. Other services are only counted by error keywords. "Likely brute force" is a simple rule (10 or more failures, or 5 or more within 5 minutes) and can include false alarms such as a mistyped password. Always verify before blocking anyone. The sample log is made up and uses documentation IP ranges.
